Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers the PalmGlitch Clips website and the "PalmGlitch Agency" publishing app, which PalmGlitch Clips uses to upload videos to the accounts of its own brands, PalmGlitch Clips and Clipialo, on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

What the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the PalmGlitch Clips and Clipialo accounts we connect.
  • Basic account and video information (channel name, video IDs, view and like counts, and combined audience statistics such as country, age group and gender) so we can see how our own videos perform.

How we use it

Only to publish our own clips and read our own performance numbers. We do not sell, share or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a private folder, and are never shared with third parties.

Google user data

PalmGlitch Agency's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are provided through the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in the settings of each platform. On request we delete stored tokens and data within 30 days.

Website visitors

This website uses no cookies, trackers or analytics.
